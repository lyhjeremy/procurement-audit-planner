Create the qa-reviewer agent for the Phase 3 agent team in CLAUDE.md, and the scripts it runs in scripts/checks/:
- check_quotes.py: runs the extract-rules verifier on rules.json against data/contract-rules.pdf, and checks every rule quote excerpt in risk-register.md matches rules.json.
- check_numbers.py: every figure of three or more digits or with a £ sign in planning-memo.md, risk-control-matrix.md and audit-program.md is a value in analytics.json, pack-figures.json, rules.json or outputs/samples/*.csv. Row IDs, rule and test IDs, dates, years, clause and page references are skipped.
- check_trace.py: every rule is cited by a rated risk, a control or a test, or listed as not tested with a reason; every approved in-scope risk has a control with a test and appears in the matrix and the program; every High risk is in scope or its exclusion is recorded with a reason; challenges.md exists, every row cites evidence, and none is open.
- check_samples.py: every transaction in outputs/samples/*.csv and every row ID in audit-program.md exists in spend-clean.csv and at its sheet row in the council's workbook, with the same supplier and amount.
- run_checks.py: runs all four and prints one table and a verdict.
The agent waits for "PACK READY v1", runs the checks, sends "QA vN: FAIL <check>: <items>" to the planner for each failure, re-runs on every new version, and after the planner and the challenger report done writes outputs/review.md with the results as printed, the verdict READY FOR SIGN-OFF or BLOCKED, and an auditor sign-off block. It fixes nothing and softens nothing.

Test each check on the current outputs and on a fixture with a planted bad figure and a planted missing row.
