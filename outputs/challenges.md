# Challenges to the audit planning pack

Packs reviewed: PACK READY v1 (round one), v2 (round two) and v3 (final check). Register revision 1, gate closed: R-01 to R-09 approved and in scope, R-10 rejected. Date: 2026-10-06.

I read the approved register and the auditor's decisions first, then checked each risk's controls, tests and samples against the summary metrics in `analytics.json` and the opinions in `history.json`. The controls cover every rule the risks cite, so the weak spots were in how samples were drawn and in what the memo left unsaid. In v1 most samples took the largest items first, which put the same few high-value suppliers into several tests and left the part of each population where the indicator is most specific unsampled. Two small populations were only partly sampled, and one rating looked low next to a metric the register did not use. I checked every accepted change in the rebuilt pack before marking it resolved. One limit remains after the changes: T-09.2 holds only 2 direct-payment items, because only 2 suppliers carry the "Direct Payment" narrative in the spend data, and children's direct payments cannot be identified at all. The memo Limitations states this. Every point below is about the plan; none is a finding about the council or any supplier.
