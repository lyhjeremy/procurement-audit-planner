# Challenges to the audit planning pack

Packs reviewed: PACK READY v1 (round one), v2 (round two) and v3 (final check). Register revision 2, gate closed: R-01 to R-09 approved and in scope (R-04 amended to cover grants under CPR-66 and agency staff; premises excluded), R-10 rejected. Date: 2026-10-09.

I read the revision 2 register and the auditor's decisions first, then checked each risk's controls, tests and samples against the summary metrics in `analytics.json` and the opinions in `history.json`. The controls cover every rule the risks cite. The weak spots in v1 were in where the samples land. The only adverse opinion on a procurement theme is AUD-16, which is on children's direct payments, but the T-09.2 sample held only adult ones. The R-04 samples skipped the £100,000 to £500,000 band. The new grant samples sat in one education funding run. Two not-tested reasons and one limitation in the memo did not hold against what the pack already requests. Round two found one wording gap left by the v2 changes. I checked every accepted change in the rebuilt pack before marking it resolved. One limit remains: T-09.3 depends on the council's own list of children's direct payments being complete, and the memo states this. Every point below is about the plan; none is a finding about the council or any supplier.
